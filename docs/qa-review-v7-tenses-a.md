## Coverage notes (vs EGU / Grammar for IELTS)

- Present Simple — form (-s/-es, do/does), meaning (habits, facts, timetables, permanent states), markers and adverb position, state verbs: covered; the only gap is the -es/-ies spelling rule (minor row).
- Present Continuous — form, -ing spelling (e-drop, doubling), meaning (now, temporary, trends, fixed future arrangements, always + annoyance), markers, state verbs: covered; gaps: -ie → -y, the “always + annoyance” point has no example/question, the future-arrangement point is A2 inside an A1 theme (minor/major rows).
- Present Perfect — form, four meanings, markers, been/gone, finished-time contrast, participle forms: covered; “this is the first time” is tested but only exemplified (minor row).
- Present Perfect Continuous — form, duration vs visible result, for/since/how long/lately/recently/all day, state-verb restriction: covered well.
- Past Simple — regular/irregular, did-negatives/questions, meaning and finished-time markers, be forms and mistakes: covered except that **was/were are not taught in the form section** (major row).
- Past Continuous — form, action in progress at a past moment/background, when/while interrupted pattern, markers, state verbs: covered except **two simultaneous continuous actions** (major row).
- Overall the six rules form a clean EGU-style progression (form → meaning → markers → mistakes) and are concise and non-overlapping; the issues are the specific gaps and the item-level defects above, not the structure.

## Summary

All 180 questions were read in full, including all 30 build questions (token multiset, accepted orders, alternative natural orders, translation and explanation) and all 30 match questions (pair mapping). The answer keys are correct throughout; there are no wrong keys and no translation/explanation defects beyond the small ones listed. The two critical items are genuine second readings (Past Continuous `laid` in tense-past-continuous-0016 and present `are` in the marker-less Past Simple item tense-past-simple-0004). The main systematic weaknesses are: two rule gaps that the items test (Past Simple was/were, Past Continuous simultaneous actions), five match questions with an ambiguous left↔right mapping, three build questions that reject standard alternative orders, four cross-type duplicate/answer-reveal pairs inside the same theme, and one ambiguous choice item (knew / have known). The remaining minors concern one more marker-less Past Simple item, two likely past-simple distractors under `while`, small rule/example/spelling gaps, two translation imperfections, mixed 3- vs 4-option choice items, fronted build orders not accepted, a few near-duplicates, and the A1 level of the future-arrangement content.
