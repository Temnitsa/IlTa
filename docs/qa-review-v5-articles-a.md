## Summary

The five themes cover the EGU/Grammar for IELTS scope correctly at a high level — countable/uncountable and generic-vs-specific overview, a/an first mention and professions, the known/unique/specification/superlatives, a/an measure/frequency/sound exceptions, and the zero article with institutions, transport, meals, time and fixed expressions. All 30 new questions have correct keys and natural translations; one is structurally broken (zero-v5-0005, critical). The two structural problems that need the most attention are (a) art-a-an: the theme mixes in ten B2 definite-article questions and a rule section that duplicate art-names-geo/art-contrasts and do not match the A1 a/an theme, and (b) art-the-known §3 teaches “the next/last” as always definite, contradicting themes 7/9 and EGU. Two rule gaps (art-system without the a/an form rule, art-a-an-special without the abbreviation rule) mean that existing/new questions test material the rules do not explain. The remaining minor items are duplicates/near-duplicates (one 0.99 duplicate across themes), stale/inconsistent tags, and wording/terminology issues.
