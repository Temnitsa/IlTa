# QA review — IlTa 3.2 prepositions (themes 1–6)

**Verdict: fail** — the 3.2 preposition rewrite is a clear improvement in format (concrete collocation lists instead of the 3.1 meta-text, and every rule example now has an aligned Russian translation), and the question bank itself is untouched and structurally sound. But the acceptance criteria are not met: four teaching points that the unchanged question bank relies on were dropped in 3.2 (spatial relations such as *next to/behind/opposite/inside*, *-ing* after prepositions, no-prep verbs *enter/reach/marry/join*, and *since*), and several English Grammar in Use 121–136 subrules are still missing from the rules (unit 124B–E place uses, 125B *at the doctor's*, 127A *in the sun/shade/love*, 135 *consist of/live on*, 136 *happen to*, etc.). No wrong answers or unsolvable matches were found. **Counts: 0 critical, 6 major, 8 minor.**

Reviewed read-only (no content/code edits, no gradle; only this report was written): `app/src/main/assets/data/content/prepositions_01_time.json` … `prepositions_06_fixed_advanced.json` — **6 rules / 29 sections / 124 rule examples with 124 exampleTranslations / 310 questions (275 choice + 35 match) / 140 match pairs**. Every rule section (heading, body ru/en, examples, exampleTranslations) and every match question was read; the choice bank was diffed against `git HEAD` (3.1.0) and spot-checked in all six themes. The whole question inventory was checked structurally (single blank, answer ∈ options, ids/levels/tags, rule mapping, explanations present). `/tmp/egiu_prepositions.txt` (units 121–136, lines ~14380–16050) was the coverage baseline. Validator: run read-only; all six preposition files pass.

2. Cross-check every preposition tested in the 310-item bank against the rule bodies (the automated word-level check used here finds rule-level blind spots); the acceptance bar is: every tested preposition appears in some rule body, and every EGU 121–136 subrule appears in at least one bullet or example.
